About

I am a Visiting Postdoctoral Researcher at KTH Royal Institute of Technology, in the Robotics, Perception and Learning division, working with Hossein Azizpour. I am also a Research and Teaching Assistant in the Department of Control and Automation Engineering at Istanbul Technical University, where I received my Ph.D. in 2025.

My research connects machine learning with optimization and dynamical systems. I develop robust learning methods for imperfect data and generative models, with a particular focus on flow matching. My interests include biomolecular modeling, robotics, and learning-based control.
