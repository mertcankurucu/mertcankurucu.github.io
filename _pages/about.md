I am a Visiting Postdoctoral Researcher at [KTH Royal Institute of Technology](https://www.kth.se/is/rpl), in the Robotics, Perception and Learning division, working with [Hossein Azizpour](https://scholar.google.com.tr/citations?user=t6CRgJsAAAAJ). I am also a Research and Teaching Assistant in the Department of Control and Automation Engineering at [Istanbul Technical University](https://www.itu.edu.tr/en), where I received my Ph.D. in 2025.

My research connects machine learning with optimization and dynamical systems. I develop robust learning methods for imperfect data and generative models, with a particular focus on flow matching. My interests include biomolecular modeling, robotics, and learning-based control.
